## ECG recordings

This prototype uses the PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2017 single-lead ECG dataset, with Normal, AF, Other Rhythm, and Noisy labels. The project's local evaluation uses a random recording-level split from the publicly available training recordings; it is not the challenge's hidden test set.

Download the permitted training files from the source below. Place `REFERENCE.csv` and the matching record `.mat` files directly in `data/ecg/`. Then run `python .\extract_ecg_features.py` to create `data/ecg/ecg_features.csv`; run `python .\train_afib_classifier.py` after that to train and save the model. Keep the source data local unless its terms allow redistribution.

- Dataset: [PhysioNet Challenge 2017](https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2017/1.0.0/)
- Required publication citation: Clifford GD et al. “AF classification from a short single lead ECG recording: the PhysioNet/computing in cardiology challenge 2017.” Computing in Cardiology, 2017. DOI: [10.22489/CinC.2017.065-469](https://doi.org/10.22489/CinC.2017.065-469)

## Synthetic EHR profiles

The profile export was produced with [Synthea](https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea). The Synthea software is Apache-2.0 licensed. The records in this project are synthetic and are not real patient records. Place `patients.csv` and `conditions.csv` in `data/synthea/csv/` for `load_synthea.py`. Preserve the Synthea attribution and check its current terms if redistributing the generated export.

## Synthetic wearable stream

Wearable readings are generated by generate_wearable_stream.py. They are artificial interface scenarios, not observations, clinical measurements, or validated physiology.

## WHO chart reference

The optional WHO South Asia calculator is a separate lookup from the WHO 2019 non-laboratory cardiovascular risk chart. It estimates combined fatal/non-fatal stroke or heart attack over 10 years; it is not a stroke-only output and does not use the simulated wearable stream.
